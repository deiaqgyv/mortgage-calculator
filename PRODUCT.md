# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Home buyers comparing financing options across the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Germany, France, and Spain.

## Product purpose

Give people a clear, private estimate of mortgage payments, total interest, remaining balance, and the cost of extra payments. Every result exposes its assumptions and local rule date.

## Personality

Clear, careful, grounded.

## Anti-references

- Generic fintech dashboards with navy gradients and oversized promises.
- Ad-heavy calculators that hide the result below the fold.
- Machine-translated country pages using the wrong local mortgage vocabulary.

## Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 AA target, keyboard-first controls, visible focus, 44px touch targets, plain-language errors, reduced-motion support, and no color-only meaning.
